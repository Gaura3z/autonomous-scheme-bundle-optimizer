# Statutory Conflict & Exclusion Matrix (PS16)

This document specifies the mutual exclusion rules enforced by the optimizer to protect citizens from statutory clawbacks and disqualifications.

| Primary Scheme | Conflicting Scheme | Statutory Reason & Gazette Clause | Resolution Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Sector Scholarship for College (NSP)** | **State Post-Matric Scholarship (SC/ST/OBC)** | NSP guidelines explicitly forbid holding two government scholarships covering tuition/maintenance. | Keep the higher annual payout; flag the exclusion clearly. |
| **PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi** | **Institutional Landholder / Govt Pension > ₹10k/mo** | Exclusion Clause B(iii) of PM-KISAN guidelines. | Automatic disqualification of PM-KISAN if pension status is detected. |
| **PM SVANidhi (Urban Vendor)** | **PMEGP Manufacturing Subsidy** | SVANidhi is micro working capital; PMEGP requires full manufacturing project setup. | SVANidhi prioritized first; PMEGP phased for future expansion. |
| **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** | **CGHS / ECHS Contributory Schemes** | PM-JAY is exclusively for non-contributory SECC beneficiaries. | Retains PM-JAY if unorganized sector; defers if formal CGHS enrolled. |
